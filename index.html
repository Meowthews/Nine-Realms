A Xianxia Cultivation Mod For Elin

Nine Realms: Path of the Dao

Open the first gate, temper flesh into scripture, refine breath into qi, and risk each breakthrough on the long ascent from mortal limits to Dao Ancestor.

Cultivation is deliberate: it drains stamina and mana, pauses at realm gates, and rewards the player who survives the backlash. The mod adds Body, Qi, and Rogue cultivation routes, shared immortal realms, techniques, arts, a journal-based menu, and an expanding lore archive.

Current Scripture

What This Mod Adds

A cultivation experience built around pacing, path identity, resource pressure, dangerous bottlenecks, and techniques that make the fantasy readable inside Elin.

Journal Access

Find Cultivation In The Player Journal

After awakening Cultivation Initiate, open the in-game player journal and use the Nine Realms entry to reach the cultivation menu. That journal route is the main doorway into the system.

Three Ways To Walk

Choose A Cultivation Identity

Formal paths gain stronger efficiencies and breakthroughs. Rogues trade certainty for breadth, advancing without crossing formal realm gates.

Body

Body Cultivator

Skin to marrow, flesh to iron. Body cultivation leans into direct physical supremacy.

  • Primary growth: STR, END, DEX, PER, WIL, PV
  • Resource reward focus: max HP and stamina
  • Realms culminate in Indestructible Body before immortality
Qi

Qi Practitioner

Breath, spirit, and hidden flow. Qi cultivation favors evasion, magical bearing, meditation, and refined reserves.

  • Primary growth: CHA, PER, LER, WIL, MAG, DV, Meditation
  • Resource reward focus: max HP and MP
  • Realms culminate in Tribulation Transcendence before immortality
Rogue

Rogue Cultivator

A wanderer without formal inheritance. Rogues can train broadly and slowly, but cannot formally break through.

  • Broad stat rolls across physical and spiritual attributes
  • Can progress to the current cap without formal gates
  • Best for flexible, uncertain, outsider-style play
Dao Paths

Arts Beyond The Three Roads

Dao paths are independent from Body, Qi, and Rogue cultivation. They follow separate requirements, progression rules, and leveling systems, allowing a cultivator's chosen Dao to grow beside their realm without replacing it.

Cultivation Structure

Realms & Stages

Every major realm spans 1000 cultivation progress. Formal paths move through Early, Middle, Late, and Peak stages, then pause at the gate until breakthrough succeeds.

  • Thresholds run from 1000 through the current 18000 cap.
  • Formal paths stop at the next threshold - 1 until breakthrough.
  • Shared immortal realms unlock after the ninth Body or Qi realm.
AbilityLong Action
Core Ability

Cultivate

To cultivate is to sit before heaven and earth, draw breath through the mortal shell, and force spirit, body, and intent into alignment.

  • Unlocked through Cultivation Initiate.
  • Each tick spends stamina and MP.
  • Realm gates create breakthrough bottlenecks.

Independent Progression

Dao Paths

Dao paths are independent from Body, Qi, and Rogue cultivation. They use their own requirements, progression rules, and leveling systems.

  • Current planned Dao paths: Sword Dao and Dao of Music.
  • Dao progression is separate from cultivation realm progression.
  • Future Dao paths may introduce their own trials and thresholds.
