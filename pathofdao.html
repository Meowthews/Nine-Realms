Cultivation systems at a glance

Nine Realms adds deliberate cultivation pacing, path identity, resource pressure, dangerous bottlenecks, techniques, arts, treasures, and an expanding lore archive inside Elin.

Shortcut Access Add Nine Realms to the hotbar, then cultivate from a dedicated mod menu.
Player Loop

Awaken, cultivate, break through, inherit.

Progression is deliberate. Cultivation drains stamina and mana, pauses at realm gates, and rewards players who survive bottlenecks, backlash, and long-term build choices.

Begin The Path
Three Ways To Walk

Choose a cultivation identity

Formal paths gain stronger efficiencies and breakthroughs. Rogues trade certainty for breadth, advancing without crossing formal realm gates.

Body Path

Body Cultivator

Body cultivation leans into direct physical supremacy, raising STR, END, DEX, PER, WIL, PV, max HP, and stamina rewards.

Realm Compass

Slide through the ascent

Use the cultivation meter to see how the current threshold curve carries a player from the first mortal realm through the shared immortal ascent.

First Steps

Getting started

Begin with the first inner gate, add Nine Realms to the shortcut bar, then cultivate until your path begins to take shape.

Gate 01

Awaken Cultivation

Obtain the Cultivation Initiate feat. This unlocks Nine Realms progression.

Gate 02

Add The Shortcut

Click an empty shortcut slot and add Nine Realms to open the cultivation menu from the hotbar.

Gate 03

Cultivate And Rise

Gather progress, raise your level, and push toward the next realm gate.

Gate 04

Choose A Path

Commit to Body, Qi, or the slower outsider road of Rogue cultivation.

Gate 05

Seek Hidden Teachings

Learn techniques, arts, and skills from rare scrolls, the Black Market, or the Eternal Exchange Pavilion.

Recovery

Repair And Restore

Use Settings tools to repair cultivation state and manage character-scoped backup snapshots.
